Gesture Field

A particle Saturn you sculpt with your hand. Its rings follow real Keplerian orbits — inner motes race, outer motes drift.

  • Open your palm to expand the field toward you. It brightens like a lamp as it grows.
  • Close to a fist to pull it back into a small, dim, distant world.
  • Push it wide open and the orbits break into a chaotic, fly-like swarm as the particles blow past the screen.
  • Drag to orbit the view · F for fullscreen.
